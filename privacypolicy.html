Your moments matter. So does your privacy. We believe that trust is part of every story we capture.

01. Introduction

Welcome to LINUS® Photography. This policy explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information when you visit our website or contact us.

02. Information We Collect

Depending on your interaction with us, we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details.
  • Preferred shoot dates and locations.
  • Photography package and session requirements.
  • Messages and information you voluntarily share.
  • Basic technical information collected by our hosting provider.

03. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Arrange photography sessions.
  • Discuss packages and customised shoots.
  • Communicate with clients.
  • Improve our website and services.

We do not sell your personal information.

04. PACE BOT

PACE BOT is a scripted assistant that helps visitors explore our services, packages and booking options.

The chatbot is designed to maintain conversation context while the chat is open, without retaining conversation history between visits.

If you continue a conversation through WhatsApp or Instagram, the privacy policies of those platforms also apply.

05. Sharing Information

We do not sell or rent personal information. Information may be handled by service providers where necessary to operate our website and communication services.

06. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. However, no online system can guarantee complete security.

07. Photography & Consent

We respect the privacy of individuals appearing in our photographs and videos. Appropriate permission will be sought before using identifiable client images for public promotional purposes, where required.

08. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, objection to certain processing, or deletion where legally applicable.

09. Contact Us
